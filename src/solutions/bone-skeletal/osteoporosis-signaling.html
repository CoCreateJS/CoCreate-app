Osteoporosis and Signaling Pathways

Summarizes molecular and cellular mechanisms associated with bone density loss and potential signaling targets explored in preclinical and clinical research.

Pathways and Targets

Wnt, RANK/RANKL/OPG, and sclerostin pathways are central to bone remodeling; multiple interventions aim to modulate these signaling nodes.

Research Highlight

Modulating Remodeling

Therapies that adjust osteoblast/osteoclast balance through signaling modulators may improve bone mass and reduce fracture risk when used alongside mechanical and nutritional strategies.
