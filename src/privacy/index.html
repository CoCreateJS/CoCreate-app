Privacy Policy for Geneomes

At Geneomes, accessible at geneomes.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and patients. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Geneomes and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through email at support@geneomes.com.

This privacy policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in Geneomes. This policy is not applicable to any information collected offline or via channels other than this website.

Key Principles:
  • We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal biological and health data.
  • We do not sell your personal genetic or health data to third parties.
  • We only collect information necessary to provide our diagnostic, peptide, and regenerative therapy services.
  • You maintain ownership of your data and can request deletion at any time, subject to medical record retention laws.
How we use your information

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information about you such as your name, email address, phone number, the contents of the message and/or attachments you may send us, and any other information you may choose to provide.

When you register for an Account or purchase a Blueprint, we may ask for your contact information, including items such as name, address, email address, and telephone number.

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our platform and services
  • Process your diagnostic tests and deliver personalized health reports
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our regenerative protocols
  • Communicate with you regarding your health plan, appointments, and updates
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • Find and prevent fraud

Like many modern platforms, Geneomes uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Geneomes's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of any third-party tools we may utilize for payment processing or scheduling for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. To know more detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers, it can be found at the browsers' respective websites.
