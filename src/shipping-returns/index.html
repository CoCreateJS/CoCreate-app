Shipping & Returns

Last update: January 23, 2026

Shipping Terms

These terms apply to all orders placed on CoCreateJS.com.

  • Processing time: Orders are typically prepared within 1 to 3 business days.
  • Shipping methods and delivery estimates are shown at checkout and vary by destination.
  • Shipping costs (including any free-shipping thresholds) are displayed before purchase.
  • Tracking details are emailed once your order ships.
  • Duties, taxes, and customs fees (if applicable) are the customer's responsibility.
  • Please ensure your address is accurate. Orders returned due to incorrect addresses may incur reshipment fees.
Returns & Refunds

If you need to return an item, contact support before sending anything back.

  • Return window: Requests must be made within 30 days of delivery.
  • Condition: Items must be unused, unopened, and in original packaging.
  • Exclusions: Final-sale items and opened consumables are not eligible.
  • Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item was damaged or incorrect.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method after inspection, typically within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Original shipping fees are non-refundable.
Issues, Changes, and Contact
  • Damaged or incorrect items: Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos.
  • Order changes or cancellations must be requested before the order ships.
  • Carrier delays can occur; if tracking has not updated for several days, reach out and we will help.
  • Questions? Email support@CoCreateJS.com.
